About MoveWell

A physiotherapy centre built on one belief: recovery should be measured, not guessed.

Care that treats the cause, not just the pain

MoveWell Physiotherapy Centre in Gulberg, Lahore, was founded by physiotherapists who were tired of rushed, machine-only treatment. Here, every patient gets a full assessment, a written plan, and one-to-one sessions with a qualified DPT therapist.

  • DPT-qualified therapists — no technicians treating you
  • One patient at a time, for the full session
  • Home visits across Lahore for patients who cannot travel
  • Written progress reports you can share with your doctor

What we stand for

Honest advice

If physiotherapy cannot help your condition, we tell you — and point you to the right specialist instead of selling sessions.

Measurable progress

Movement, strength and pain scores are recorded and re-checked. You see the numbers improve, session by session.

Care that comes to you

Stroke, post-surgery and elderly patients recover faster at home. Our therapists visit across Lahore with full portable equipment.
